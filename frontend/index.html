      How to play

      Your runner never stops. Press space, click or tap to jump, and keep holding to jump higher. Let go early for a short hop over crates.

      Crashing into a wall or a crate ends the run, and so does falling between buildings. The further you get, the faster the city goes by.

      Keyboard: space, up arrow or W to jump, P to pause, M to mute.

      Portals and worlds

      Every so often a glowing portal appears on a rooftop. Run into it and you are pulled into a random hidden world, such as an abandoned apartment, underground tunnels or an industrial tunnel.

      Collect coins along the way. Spend them on hats and scarves, or on a revive when a run ends too soon.

      About the game

      Roof Top Rift is a free browser game made by a student developer. There is nothing to download or install, and it runs on any modern phone, tablet or computer. Post your best distance to the leaderboard and see how far other runners got.
