What the game stores

Your best distance, your coins, the costumes you own, your sound setting and the last name you typed are saved in your own browser (local storage). They never leave your device unless you post a score.

Leaderboard

When you post a score we store the name you typed, your distance and the time. Please do not use your real full name or any personal details. Your IP address is used briefly to limit spam and is not saved with your score.
